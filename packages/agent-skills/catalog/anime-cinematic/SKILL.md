# Anime Cinematic

Preserve the creator's subject, project facts, approved references, identity, exact copy, delivery constraints, and safety policy. Apply only the visual direction required by the requested artifact.

## Visual Grammar

- Start from a strong layout: clear depth planes, purposeful perspective, cinematic crop, and readable negative space.
- Combine clean character linework and controlled cel shading with atmospheric painted environments.
- Use light, weather, particles, and environmental motion to support story mood instead of decorative overload.
- Direct action with strong keys, impact poses, held emotional beats, and stable character identity.

## Prompt Authoring

<!-- fizzdragon:policy:prompt:start -->

Use contemporary cinematic anime language: authored perspective layouts, clean expressive linework, controlled cel shadow shapes, luminous atmospheric backgrounds, purposeful environmental motion, strong key poses, and emotionally legible acting. Avoid generic fan-art composition, excessive effects, unstable facial design, and indiscriminate speed lines.

## Image Generation

<!-- fizzdragon:policy:image:start -->

Create a contemporary anime film frame with a deliberate cinematic layout, clean character linework, controlled cel shading, atmospheric painted background depth, motivated luminous light, restrained environmental particles, and a clear emotional gesture. Keep anatomy, costume details, and facial identity stable; avoid glossy 3D rendering.

<!-- fizzdragon:policy:image:end -->

## Video Generation

<!-- fizzdragon:policy:video:start -->

Animate through strong readable key poses, purposeful holds, expressive timing, stable facial design, coherent cel shadows, environmental follow-through, and motivated camera movement. Use impact frames or smears sparingly and end on a clean composed key pose.

## Boundaries

- Do not override explicit creator requirements, approved references, permissions, safety policy, model capability, or tool contracts.
- Do not invent assets, approvals, project facts, dialogue, inspected evidence, or completed generation results.
- Do not add executable tools, third-party credentials, or direct provider calls.
- Use original characters and art direction; do not reproduce protected anime designs the creator did not provide.
