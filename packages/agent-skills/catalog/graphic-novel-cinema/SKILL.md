# Graphic Novel Cinema

Preserve the creator's subject, project facts, approved references, identity, exact copy, delivery constraints, and safety policy. Apply only the visual direction required by the requested artifact.

## Visual Grammar

- Organize every frame through strong black, midtone, and light masses before surface detail.
- Use purposeful ink contours, hatching, dry-brush texture, and selective color accents to control attention.
- Compose with panel-aware crops, foreground silhouettes, graphic perspective, and readable speech-safe negative space when needed.
- Translate motion through pose, silhouette, parallax, animated texture, and restrained panel transitions without flattening spatial continuity.

## Prompt Authoring
