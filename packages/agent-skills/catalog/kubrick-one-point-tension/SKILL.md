## Prompt Authoring

Build psychological tension through one-point perspective, architectural repetition, deliberate negative space, emotionally cool lighting, exact blocking, and controlled pacing. Keep the language original and grounded in scene intent.

## Image Generation

<!-- fizzdragon:policy:image:start -->
