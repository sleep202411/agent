# Cinematic Visual Direction

## Goal

Keep every generation visually consistent while preserving the user's subject,
story facts, reference assets, and explicit requirements.

## Creative instructions

1. Establish a clear visual hierarchy before adding decorative detail.
2. Keep the subject identity, wardrobe, props, and environment consistent across shots.
3. Describe lighting, palette, composition, lens language, and atmosphere concretely.
4. For video, add camera movement, subject motion, pacing, and continuity constraints.
5. Prefer production-ready directions over abstract adjectives.

## Preserve

- Explicit user requirements and project canon
- Approved character and environment references
- Readable silhouettes and intentional composition
- Continuity between adjacent images or shots

## Avoid

- Unrequested changes to identity, costume, location, or story facts
- Contradictory camera or lighting directions
- Empty style buzzwords without visible consequences
- Text, logos, watermarks, or extra objects unless requested
